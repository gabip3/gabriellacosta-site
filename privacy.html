Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

Overview

This policy explains what information gabriellacosta.design ("this site," "we") collects, how it's used, and your choices. This site is operated by Gabriella Costa, an independent web design studio based in São Paulo, Brazil.

Information you provide

When you fill out a contact or quote form, we collect what you enter yourself — typically your name, email address, phone/WhatsApp number, and the message or project details you share. This information is used only to respond to your inquiry and discuss your project. We don't sell or rent it to third parties.

Form submissions are processed through Web3Forms, a third-party form-delivery service that forwards your message to our inbox. If you submit a lead form through a Google ad, the information you provide is shared with us by Google as described below.

Information collected automatically

Like most websites, we use a few tools to understand how visitors use the site and to measure the performance of our advertising:

  • Google Analytics — aggregate usage data (pages viewed, general location, device type).
  • Google Ads — conversion tracking, to measure which ads lead to inquiries.
  • Microsoft Clarity — anonymized session recordings and heatmaps, to see how people navigate the site and improve it.

These tools may use cookies or similar technology. None of them are used to sell your personal data.

Your choices

You can ask us at any time to see, correct, or delete the information you've shared with us — just email gabip3@gmail.com. You can also control cookies and tracking through your browser's privacy settings.

Data retention

We keep inquiry messages only as long as needed to respond to you and, if you become a client, to deliver the project. You can request deletion at any time.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Reach out at gabip3@gmail.com or via the contact form.
